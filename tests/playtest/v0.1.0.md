# Murim Life Game v0.1.0 · Owner Playtest Script

Run on the Windows standalone release build (`Builds/Windows/MurimLifeGame.exe`) at `1920 × 1080` unless stated otherwise. Each test lists setup, steps, evidence to note, and the pass condition from `docs/specs/v0.1.0-technical.md` §6.5.

---

## PT-1 · Direct Launch and Clean Screen (`RC-1`)

- **Setup:** Launch `Builds/Windows/MurimLifeGame.exe` directly on a `1920 × 1080` display. Do not touch mouse or keyboard for 10 seconds after the valley appears.
- **Steps:**
  1. Launch the executable and observe the first rendered frame and the next 10 seconds.
- **Evidence to note:** Initial camera view, capsule presence in the lower clearing, absence of any HUD, floor label, destination marker, menu, or audio, and the player log containing only `[Murim]` game lines free of local paths, usernames, or machine names.
- **Pass condition:** Launch goes straight to the valley in Full View with the ivory capsule standing in the lower clearing; nothing else appears on screen for 10 seconds, and no audio plays.

## PT-2 · Natural Hand-Built Composition (`RC-2`)

- **Setup:** Running valley from `PT-1` in Full View at default zoom (`11.25`).
- **Steps:**
  1. Pan from the west edge to the east edge at default zoom, counting approximate default screen widths across the valley.
  2. Pan north to the north crest and south to the river, inspecting the river channel, riffles, river rocks, plunge pool, waterfall and foam, lower clearing, exposed earth patches, granite outcrops, sheer cliff bands, slopes S1–S4, upper terraces, Lone terrace, dense bamboo grove, Hidden glade, mountain pines, boulders, shrubs, grass, and wildflowers.
- **Evidence to note:** Approximate valley width in default screen widths, confirmation of every listed natural element, and any visual defect.
- **Pass condition:** Panning edge to edge at default zoom covers about 4 screen widths and reaches the north crest; every listed natural element is found; nothing man-made, no path-like ribbon of earth, and no rock group that reads as a river crossing appears.

## PT-3 · Repeatable Fixed Layout and Behavior (`RC-3`)

- **Setup:** Fresh launch at default start state `(70.5, 28.5)`.
- **Steps:**
  1. From the start position, issue three distinct left-clicks in the lower clearing (e.g., west meadow, near river bank, and up slope S1 onto Floor 2), letting each walk finish. Note the three stopping locations.
  2. Close the application, relaunch `MurimLifeGame.exe`, and repeat the exact same three clicks.
- **Evidence to note:** Visual landmarks at the three stopping spots across both launches.
- **Pass condition:** After relaunch, the same three clicks from the start end at the same three spots and the valley is unchanged.

## PT-4 · Multi-Level Floor Stepping, Full View, and Slicing (`RC-4`)

- **Setup:** Valley in Full View. Optionally walk the placeholder onto Floor 2 (up Slope S1) or ascend to `Air Layer 1` to verify slicing of the placeholder as well.
- **Steps:**
  1. Press `PageDown` (or `[`) five times from Full View.
  2. Press `PageUp` (or `]`) five times from Ground Floor 1.
  3. Wait after each input (or at the end) to confirm the label fades out completely.
  4. While at `Ground Floor 1`, inspect where upper terraces, tall pine canopies, and a placeholder above band 1 were located.
- **Evidence to note:** Exact label strings displayed (`Ground Floor 4`, `Ground Floor 3`, `Ground Floor 2`, `Ground Floor 1`, `Full View`), limit refreshes, fade completion, and solid cut cap appearance.
- **Pass condition:** `PageDown` from Full View shows `Ground Floor 4`, then `3`, `2`, `1`, then refreshes `Ground Floor 1`; `PageUp` returns through each floor to `Full View` and refreshes there; each label fades fully; at floor 1 the terraces, canopies, and a capsule on a terrace are gone and cut ground looks solid.

## PT-5 · Fixed-Angle Camera Controls, Bounds, and Focus Loss (`RC-5`)

- **Setup:** Valley in Full View.
- **Steps:**
  1. Pan in all directions with `WASD` and Arrow keys, including diagonals.
  2. Hold opposing keys together (`W` + `S`, `A` + `D`) and check horizontal/vertical cancellation.
  3. Pan with right-mouse drag and middle-mouse drag.
  4. Hold a pan key while dragging with right-mouse, then release the drag while still holding the key.
  5. Zoom all the way in and all the way out with the mouse scroll wheel, and pan to all four valley edges at close, default, and widest zoom.
  6. Move the camera away from the capsule (and optionally slice below the capsule's floor), press `Space`, then pan/zoom/slice away and press `Home`.
  7. Hold a pan key or drag, `Alt+Tab` away to lose window focus, observe ambient motion while unfocused, then return focus without pressing new keys.
- **Evidence to note:** Camera angle stability, boundary clamping at edges and zoom limits, `Space` recenter + visibility restore, `Home` vista reset without moving the capsule, and post-refocus stillness.
- **Pass condition:** Every camera control works; the angle never changes; no void at any edge or zoom; opposing keys cancel; keys plus drag do not double; alt-tabbing away mid-pan leaves the view still on return while ambient motion continued.

## PT-6 · Ground Traversal, Slope Routing, and Barriers (`RC-6`)

- **Setup:** Placeholder on foot at the lower clearing in Full View.
- **Steps:**
  1. Left-click across the clearing and directly onto Floor 2 (across a sheer cliff segment where Slope S1 connects Floor 1 to Floor 2); observe that no floor label appears during the walk.
  2. From Floor 1, click onto each barrier/unreachable type: sheer cliff face, river channel, waterfall, boulder, pine trunk, Lone terrace (unconnected), and outside the walkable perimeter.
  3. Step the view to `Ground Floor 1` while the placeholder is on Floor 1, then click the top of Slope S1 / Floor 2 and watch it walk up Slope S1.
- **Evidence to note:** Route taken via slope, absence of floor label on slope walk, reachable vs. unreachable destination marker shapes/colors, clean stopping without clipping, and automatic view step-up to `Ground Floor 2` without a label when walking up Slope S1.
- **Pass condition:** Clicking a terrace walks the capsule up a slope with no label; each barrier type stops it cleanly with the amber marker; walking up S1 while viewing floor 1 raises the view to floor 2 without a label.

## PT-7 · Airborne Layers, Height Cue, and Landing Rules (`RC-7`)

- **Setup:** Placeholder on foot in the lower clearing, Full View.
- **Steps:**
  1. Press `R` three times to step through `Air Layer 1` (5 m), `Air Layer 2` (11 m), and `Air Layer 3` (17 m), then press `R` a fourth time to verify limit label refresh.
  2. Fly across a sheer cliff onto the Lone terrace and press `F` to land there.
  3. Ascend and fly over the dense bamboo grove into the Hidden glade and land.
  4. Ascend and fly out over the river channel; observe the height cue line and surface ring over water, slopes, boulders, and cliffs.
  5. Press `F` while over water, over a boulder, and over the dense bamboo grove; then move over a gentle slope (e.g., S1) and press `F`.
  6. Verify `Air Layer 3` visually clears the north crest and bamboo tops, and fly back to the main clearing.
- **Evidence to note:** Label text for each `R`/`F` press, cue line + ring visibility and placement on `SurfaceAt`, refusal to land inside obstacles/water, slope landing, and `Air Layer 3` clearance over the ridge.
- **Pass condition:** The capsule crosses a cliff onto the Lone terrace, the bamboo into the Hidden glade, and the river and back by air; height and the point beneath stay readable; `Air Layer 3` is clearly above the ridge; `F` over water, boulders, and bamboo refuses to land; `F` over a slope lands on it.

## PT-8 · System Menu Pause, Hold-Then-Finish Ascent, and Actions (`RC-8`)

- **Setup:** Placeholder in the lower clearing, Full View.
- **Steps:**
  1. Start a long walk across the clearing and press `Esc` mid-walk. Verify capsule, water, and foliage freeze immediately, and verify no dimming veil obscures the valley.
  2. While the menu is open, click on the valley outside the panel and press exploration keys (`WASD`, `R`, `F`, `PageUp`, `PageDown`, `Space`, `Home`); verify nothing responds.
  3. Click `Resume` (and repeat opening with `Esc` and closing with `Esc`); verify the interrupted walk stays cancelled and no marker was created at the button click position.
  4. Press `R` to begin an ascent and immediately press `Esc` mid-ascent; verify the capsule holds mid-air while paused, then close the menu and verify it finishes rising to `TargetElevation`.
  5. Pan/zoom/step to Floor 1 away from the capsule, open `Esc` menu, and click `Reset View`; verify default vista and Full View restore while the capsule remains at its current position.
  6. Open `Esc` menu and click `Quit`.
- **Evidence to note:** Freeze behavior, mid-ascent hold-then-finish, no click-through on `Resume` or `Reset View`, no keyboard navigation (`Enter`/`Space`) triggering buttons, and clean desktop exit on `Quit`.
- **Pass condition:** `Esc` mid-walk freezes capsule, water, and foliage; `Esc` mid-ascent holds the capsule in mid-air and it finishes rising after the menu closes; valley clicks do nothing; `Resume` and `Esc` leave the walk cancelled; `Reset View` restores the vista without moving the capsule; `Quit` exits to the desktop.

## PT-9 · Rapid Input Replacement (`RC-9`)

- **Setup:** Placeholder on foot in the lower clearing.
- **Steps:**
  1. Issue 5+ rapid consecutive left-clicks in different directions across the clearing (and some onto unreachable terrain).
  2. Issue 5+ rapid consecutive `PageDown`/`PageUp` presses and `R`/`F` presses.
- **Evidence to note:** Single active destination marker updating immediately without stacking, capsule immediately redirecting to the final target without visiting stale targets, and single active floor label updating immediately without overlapping text.
- **Pass condition:** Rapid clicks and rapid `PageUp`/`PageDown`/`R`/`F` never show two markers or two labels and never leave the capsule heading to an old target.

## PT-10 · Living Natural Atmosphere (`RC-10`)

- **Setup:** Launch in Full View and observe the valley continuously for 2 minutes (panning to view river, waterfall, bamboo, pines, grass, and wildflowers).
- **Steps:**
  1. Observe all six ambient families (river flow, waterfall cascade/foam, bamboo sway, pine foliage sway, grass sway, wildflower sway) over 2 minutes.
  2. Confirm pine trunks, boulders, and shrubs remain stationary.
  3. Confirm lighting and shadow angle never shift, and zero audio plays.
- **Evidence to note:** Subtle desynchronized sway across vegetation, steady river/waterfall motion, constant neutral daylight, and silence.
- **Pass condition:** Over 2 minutes: water, waterfall, bamboo, pines, grass, and flowers move subtly and never in unison; light never changes; no sound.

## PT-12 · Movement, Pan, and Zoom Feel (`Feel`)

- **Setup:** Exploration session across lower clearing, slopes, terraces, and air layers using default `exploration.json` and `presentation.json`.
- **Steps:**
  1. Walk across the default view width (~8 s) and across the clearing.
  2. Ascend/descend layers (~0.6–0.75 s) and fly across the air bounds (~18 s).
  3. Pan across the valley and scroll through zoom notches.
- **Evidence to note:** Subjective pacing notes and any specific field names in `exploration.json` or `presentation.json` proposed for retuning after v0.1.0 acceptance.
- **Pass condition:** Walk, fly, pan, and zoom speeds feel unhurried but not slow; note any value to retune in the two JSON files.
