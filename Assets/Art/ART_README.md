The owner separately authorized scripted resizing, positioning, seamless preparation, and original masks, rings and UI shapes.

The image sources are AI-generated, not third-party stock. No CC0, public-domain, or exclusive-copyright claim is made. Use is subject to the applicable OpenAI terms and the project owner's rights. No external artwork or font is included in this delivery.
