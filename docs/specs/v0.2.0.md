# Murim Life Game v0.2.0 · Fixed Hand-Sculpted Valley & Landscaped Sect Grounds

**Status:** Approved

**Platform:** Windows (x64) · **Engine:** Unity · **Input:** Keyboard and mouse

**Release goal.** Explore a large, fixed, hand-sculpted Chinese-wuxia mountain valley centered on landscaped, terraced sect grounds—aligned with the owner's reference composition and presented in strictly 2D painterly oblique art—using keyboard and right-mouse panning and cursor-anchored zooming.

## Intent and feel

- **Handcrafted Jianghu sanctuary with room to grow.** Launching into the valley should feel like stepping into a carefully composed classical Chinese-wuxia painting—immediately recognizable from the owner's reference composition, anchored by the gnarled focal pine in its stone-walled courtyard pond, the stone retaining walls, balustrades, lantern posts, and stairs stepping up to the main terrace, the arched wooden footbridge over the foreground stream, the right-side waterfall tumbling out of rocky bamboo hills, and inviting open clearings awaiting future sect halls and disciples.
- **Expansive, enclosed mountain wilderness.** Pulling out and panning across a regional valley spanning at least 4 screens across should convey a secluded mountain domain nestled deep inside untouched forested slopes, pine woods, rocky ridges, and bamboo groves—completely framed by natural mountain borders with no sky strip or map edge ever breaking the illusion.
- **Unmistakable multi-level verticality in 2D.** Even in strictly 2D art with a fixed oblique camera, every elevation shift—retaining walls, stone staircases, hillside slopes, the wooden bridge spanning the lower stream, the cascading waterfall, and consistent front-to-back depth sorting—must read immediately as layered vertical space ready for future structures and vertical movement.
- **Composed, familiar camera feel.** Exploration retains `v0.1.0`'s unhurried, damped panning, zoom-scaled movement, and cursor-anchored zooming so inspecting stonework and wildflowers up close feels as effortless as surveying the broader regional valley.

## Design principles

1. **Hand-sculpted permanence over procedural randomness.** The world is a single authored, fixed landscape so every terrace, waterway, path, and landmark sits intentionally where players and future sect systems expect it; every launch opens the identical valley.
2. **Prepared grounds before construction.** `v0.2.0` establishes the fixed valley's terrain, waterways, terraces, stonework, stairs, pathways, central pine courtyard, and bridge as a static foundation, leaving buildings, disciples, and simulation mechanics to future versions.
3. **Multi-level 2D elevation foundation.** All visuals remain strictly 2D painted art (zero 3D models) backed by genuine multi-level elevation tracking so the fixed valley stays ready for future player-built structures, bridges, and vertical (XYZ) movement and selection.
4. **Pure Chinese-wuxia painterly cohesion.** All natural and architectural grounds follow classical Chinese-wuxia / Jianghu traditions exclusively (never Korean Murim influences), using the owner's reference image as visual inspiration only (never shipped as a runtime asset).
5. **Fully enclosed, unobstructed exploration.** Untouched outer mountain wilderness encloses the valley on every side with no exposed void or sky strip, and with no gameplay HUD or active units yet, the entire viewport belongs to the valley.

## Controls

### While exploring

| ID | Input | Expected behavior |
|---|---|---|
| CE-1 | `W` / `A` / `S` / `D` or `Up` / `Left` / `Down` / `Right` arrow keys | Pans the camera continuously across the fixed valley along screen-aligned horizontal directions while held, scaling travel to zoom level so screen-space movement stays consistent, and easing gently to rest on release without snapping or stuttering. Diagonal panning matches cardinal perceived speed. |
| CE-2 | Right-mouse button hold + drag | Drags the camera continuously across the valley in direct 1-to-1 response to pointer motion across all zoom levels without jumping or stuttering; releasing the right mouse button ends the drag and eases to rest with gentle damping. Middle-mouse drag has no effect. |
| CE-3 | Mouse scroll wheel up / down | Zooms continuously toward the world point beneath the cursor—inward for close-up stonework/ground detail (scroll up) or outward for wide regional valley overview (scroll down) with gentle damping—halting cleanly at closest and widest zoom limits. |
| CE-4 | `Home` or `R` | Gradually eases the camera back to the default starting view (centered on the heart of the landscaped sect grounds at a balanced mid-overview zoom) without an abrupt snap. |
| CE-5 | `Esc` | Opens the minimal system overlay displaying `Paused`, `Resume`, and `Quit`, blocking world camera input while keeping the valley visible behind it. |

### While system overlay is open

| CS-2 | Left-click `Quit` | Exits the game cleanly to the Windows desktop. |

### Window and OS controls (anytime)

| ID | Input | Expected behavior |

## Edge cases

| ID | Situation | What the player experiences |
|---|---|---|
| EC-1 | Holding opposite pan keys simultaneously (e.g., `A` and `D`, or `W` and `S`) | Opposing inputs cancel cleanly so the camera holds steady on that axis without jitter while perpendicular pan keys continue working normally. |
| EC-2 | Pressing keyboard pan keys while actively right-mouse dragging | Camera motion stays predictable without compounding speeds or jumps; releasing either input transitions cleanly to the remaining active input or eases to rest. |
| EC-3 | Panning or zooming out near any outer edge or corner of the fixed valley | Camera position clamps directly along the enclosed valley borders without bouncing or jittering so neither empty void nor an open sky strip is ever visible, even at widest overview zoom. |
| EC-4 | Scrolling past the closest or widest zoom limit | Zoom halts cleanly at the limit without bouncing, overshooting, flipping orientation, or altering the fixed oblique 2D view. |
| EC-5 | Zooming in closely over layered landmarks (central stone-walled pine pond, retaining walls, balustrades, lantern posts, staircases, wooden bridge over the stream, waterfall cascade, dense pine/bamboo canopies) | All 2D painted surfaces and sprites stay crisp and maintain consistent front-to-back draw order by ground position and elevation tier, with zero flickering, popping, or foreground elements drawing behind background terrain or props. |
| EC-6 | Resting or moving the pointer along any edge of the game window | Nothing happens—screen-edge scrolling is disabled. |
| EC-7 | Window loses focus (`Alt-Tab`) or pointer leaves the window while panning or right-dragging | All active pan and drag inputs stop cleanly; on return, the camera is stationary with no stuck movement or drift. |
| EC-8 | Resizing or maximizing the window, or changing display aspect ratio | The camera stays clamped within enclosed valley bounds with no empty void or sky strip exposed, and the `Esc` system overlay (when open) remains centered and legible. |
| EC-9 | Clicking, right-dragging, scrolling, or pressing movement keys while the `Esc` overlay is open | World camera inputs are blocked behind the overlay; only `Resume`, `Quit`, `Esc`, and standard window controls respond. |
| EC-10 | Launching the game multiple times (including if any legacy external seed argument is supplied) | Every launch opens the exact same hand-sculpted fixed valley with identical terrain, landmarks, and wilderness; no procedural re-rolling or seed-driven variation occurs. |
| EC-11 | Pressing `Home` or `R` while actively panning or zooming | The reset immediately takes over from the active pan or zoom and gradually eases the camera back to the default starting view over the central sect grounds. |
| EC-12 | Pressing any pan key, right-dragging, or scrolling while a `Home`/`R` reset is still easing | The reset immediately cancels and hands control back to the player without snapping or jumping. |
| EC-13 | Left-clicking anywhere in the world (including on the central pine pond, staircases, lantern posts, bridge, pathways, or empty terrace pads) | Nothing happens—no camera motion, selection ring, tooltip, marker, or audio occurs. |
| EC-14 | Minimizing the game window while holding pan keys or right-dragging, then restoring it | Minimizing cancels all active pan and drag inputs cleanly; restoring resumes at the same camera position with no drift, stuck input, or visual distortion. |

## Out of scope

- **Procedural world generation & external seed overrides** (permanently removed in `v0.2.0` in favor of the hand-sculpted fixed valley).
- **Open sky backdrop / distant horizon strip** (valley is fully enclosed on all sides by untouched outer mountain wilderness).
- **3D models, 3D meshes, and dynamic 3D lighting** (strictly 2D painted art).
- **Buildings and functional structures** (main sect hall, thatched huts, well, market stall, garden house/trellises, incense pavilion, walled forge, and player building/placement mechanics).
- **Additional outer-valley man-made landmarks or roads** (outer valley beyond the central sect grounds is untouched natural wilderness).
- **Playable or movable units, disciples, and NPCs** (including selection rings, movement orders, pathfinding, and future XYZ flying movement/selection).
- **Gameplay HUD & time/cultivation simulation** (top Day/Paused bar, disciple portrait card, cultivation/health/qi bars, meditation actions, minimap, day progression).
- **World inspection tooltips and landmark interaction** (all grounds, stonework, stairs, pathways, and bridge elements are non-interactive scenery in `v0.2.0`).
- **Ambient environmental animations & VFX** (flowing stream motion, animated waterfall/mist, rising smoke, swaying trees/bamboo).
- **Korean Murim cultural or architectural influences** (permanently excluded; classical Chinese wuxia / Jianghu only).
- **Camera rotation, middle-mouse drag, and screen-edge scrolling.**
- **Title screen, settings menu, save/load, custom cursor, and audio.**
