# Project Vision

## Setting & Tone

A single-player Unity game set in the martial world of **Murim**—a grounded martial society of sects, disciples,
training grounds, rival schools, mountain valleys, and cultivation.

- **Murim:** martial society, sect identity, discipline, rank, training grounds, grounded clothing and equipment.
- **Wuxia:** wandering martial artists, mountain monasteries, courtyards, bamboo groves, stone paths, rivers, and
  restrained displays of extraordinary martial skill.
- **Xuanhuan:** cultivation realms, spirit stones, seclusion, and supernatural phenomena—used deliberately where
  explicitly approved in [docs/scope.md](scope.md).

## Core Experience Pillars

*(To be confirmed and expanded by the owner.)*

1. **Readable, atmospheric martial world** at the game's working camera angle and zoom.
2. **Clear player feedback** for every action, state, and outcome.
