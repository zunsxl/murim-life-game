# Project Vision

## Setting & Tone

A single-player Unity game set in a classical **Chinese wuxia / Jianghu** martial world—a grounded martial society of
sects, disciples, training grounds, rival schools, mountain valleys, and later approved Chinese cultivation. (Per
[docs/decisions.md](decisions.md), the project follows classical Chinese wuxia / Jianghu traditions exclusively and
never uses Korean Murim influences.)

- **Jianghu / Martial Society:** sect identity, discipline, rank, training grounds, grounded clothing and equipment.
- **Wuxia:** wandering martial artists, mountain monasteries, courtyards, bamboo groves, stone paths, rivers, and
  restrained displays of extraordinary martial skill.
- **Cultivation / Xuanhuan:** cultivation realms, spirit stones, seclusion, and supernatural phenomena—used deliberately
  where explicitly approved in [docs/scope.md](scope.md).

## Core Experience Pillars

*(To be confirmed and expanded by the owner.)*

1. **Readable, atmospheric martial world** at the game's working camera angle and zoom.
2. **Deterministic, reproducible simulation** so seeds and mechanics behave predictably.
3. **Clear player feedback** for every action, state, and outcome.
